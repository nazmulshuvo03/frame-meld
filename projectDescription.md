# FrameMeld: AI Story to Video Generator

## Project Description

FrameMeld is a web project where you can write your story and build a video out from it using AI, but will build everything hand in hand with AI.
The process is,

1. You write your story in a text format.
2. The AI will analyze your story and generate a storyboard or screenplay or script based on the content of your story. You will be able to edit and modify the generated content as you wish.
3. AI would also generate a list of characters with detail description of those characters.
4. You will be able to generate images for all of  your characters from those descriptions using AI image generation tools.
5. Once you have your characters and storyboard ready, the AI will help you create a video by generating scenes based on your storyboard and character images.
